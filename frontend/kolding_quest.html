Research & Strategy

Kolding Quest

Gamified intergenerational placemaking — bridging youth and elderly through urban play, shared heritage, and community connection.

Part 2

Building a Bigger Hypothesis

Context — Connection Matters
The Opportunity

Kolding possesses a balanced age distribution — a demographic dividend waiting to be activated.

The Challenge

Severe lack of cross-generational interaction leaves age groups isolated from one another.

The Goal

Activate the demographic dividend to build urban resilience and sustainable placemaking.

Strategy — Ecosystem Extension over Reinvention
Approach

Implement a location-based, gamified application module layered onto existing infrastructure.

Key Principle

Do NOT replace Kolding's existing official app. Act as an "Ecosystem Extension" — injecting new community engagement without increasing user friction.

Overarching Hypothesis & Sub-Hypotheses
Overarching Hypothesis

Transforming the city into an interactive canvas that bridges youth tech-savviness with elderly heritage knowledge will significantly enhance social cohesion.

SH1 · Youth & Economy

Digital coupons and hidden "nice spot" recommendations will drive youth outdoors while revitalizing local merchants.

SH2 · Event Visibility

Location-Based Services triggering pop-up notifications for nearby events will break information bubbles and combat fragmented interests.

SH3 · Community Breakthrough

Geolocation broadcasting of local group activities will turn closed gatherings into open events, driving organic participation.

Part 3

Proving the Hypothesis — Future Roadmap

Validation through Intergenerational Modules
A

Digital Memory Checkpoints

Gamified Heritage
  • Location-based checkpoints at historical spots across the city.
  • Youth unlock rewards by interacting with elderly volunteers at "History Cafés".
  • Fosters organic cultural exchange between generations.
B

Co-Creation Quests

Community Kitchens
  • In-app collaborative quests for youth to gather local ingredients.
  • Quests conclude at real-world community kitchens.
  • Elderly residents guide youth in cooking traditional recipes.
C

Tech-Assisted Walks

Digital Bridges
  • Elderly define "slow-pace" routes based on daily routines.
  • Youth join routes to earn points as "digital bridges".
  • Youth help elderly troubleshoot tech issues in a low-pressure setting.
